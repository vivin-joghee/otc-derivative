Interpretation

Why so few COMPLIANT. Of the 8 distinct counterparty LEIs in the portfolio, 3 fail ISO 7064 MOD 97-10 (2138002TXD6KSZ3V5X27, 9695009AXSRNHZE85Y20, 4R3ZURLYISNNNMHMK608). Only 3 of 25 conventional trades have both counterparties' LEIs valid (T013, T017, T021); T013 has a date-only execution_timestamp, T021 has maturity_date: "9999-99-99". T017 is the lone CFTC pass. T017 then fails MAS because its three collateral / margin fields are explicit null. The engine is doing what it is supposed to — surfacing the dataset's deliberate data-quality issues as legitimate compliance failures, not silently masking them.

Jurisdictional asymmetry for prediction contracts. T026, T027 and T028 are all binary event contracts with measurable economic exposure (a German renewable-subsidy hedge, an EU bond-portfolio inflation hedge, a FinTech compliance-cost hedge), but their reporting status flips entirely on venue. T026 and T028 trade on Kalshi (a CFTC-regulated DCM) → CFTC CONDITIONAL pending ANPR 91 FR 12516. T027 trades on Polymarket via VPN bypass → CFTC NOT_APPLICABLE. All three are MAS NOT_APPLICABLE. The instrument economics are unchanged; the regulatory perimeter is determined by where the order book sits and how the user accessed it.

MAS adds collateral / margin obligations CFTC does not. Of trades where every shared CDE field passes, MAS additionally requires collateral_portfolio_code, initial_margin_posted, variation_margin_posted — and a value of 0 is fine, it is null that fails. Trades T003, T004, T014, T017, T019, T020, T023, T025 that would otherwise pass CFTC's identifier and date checks fall over MAS because these three fields are simply absent from the trade record. The asset-class panel makes the cleared / collateralised trade types (Rates and Credit) visually compliant for MAS at a much higher rate than the typically uncleared FX and Equity ones.
